# Loom Chickpea Rice Bowl

## info
* Total preparation time: 20 minutes
* Servings: 2

## ingredients
* 250 g drained canned chickpeas
* 200 g cooked rice
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp ground cumin

## steps
1. Heat the olive oil in a pan.
2. Add the drained chickpeas and ground cumin.
3. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring regularly.
4. Add the already cooked rice and stir for another 2 minutes.
5. Divide into 2 servings.
